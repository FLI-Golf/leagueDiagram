# Approved narration sheet

This is the final approved copy for the three-part demo series. Use this as the source text for recording and final renders.

## Part 1 — League Admin setup

"Welcome to LeagueDiagram. We are starting from the league admin view, where the season setup is already partly mapped out. We switch accounts to League Admin and confirm the active user is now in the admin role. From the dashboard, we open Manage league and review the Create upcoming season form. The season name is already prepared, the purse amount is set, and the title sponsor is populated for us. Scrolling down, we see the rest of the season setup and tournament structure, including the upcoming tournament schedule and the timing details that keep the league ready for launch. This screen shows how the platform brings the campaign, the budget, and the calendar together before the season goes live."

### Screen alignment notes
- Start on the app landing view with Mock current user visible.
- Switch the active account to League Admin.
- Navigate to Manage league.
- Focus on the Create upcoming season form.
- Highlight Season name, Purse amount, and Title sponsor name.
- Scroll to the Season setup & tournaments section.

## Part 2 — Scorekeeper workflow

"Before we score the round, we first mock a scorekeeper and assign that role to the group so the scoring workflow is ready to go. We open the scorekeeper scorecard and review the details for each hole, including the hole number, the course setup, and the scoring inputs for every player in the group. On the first hole, we confirm the details and enter the live values before moving forward. After we advance to hole 2, we follow the same process again, referencing the previous hole and continuing the same review so the scoring progression stays consistent from one hole to the next. We update the score entries, confirm the changes, and then submit them to move to the next hole."

### Screen alignment notes
- Start on the app landing view with Mock current user visible.
- Switch the active account to a scorekeeper profile such as Ava Park.
- Open the Scorekeeper scorecard view.
- Review per-hole course details and score inputs.
- Highlight the first-hole state and then advance to hole 2.
- Adjust a few scores and submit them to move to the next hole.

## Part 3 — Switch to Fantasy Owner and seed the draft

"Now we switch to the Fantasy Owner account and confirm the role change. In this mock demo, we seed the fantasy league with participants to show how the FLI Golf fantasy draft works. We scroll down and seed the draft, then click Sunset Open to focus in on the tournament we are drafting. We highlight the draft order cards and the live state at the top of the board. Fantasy Owner is on the clock, with no picks yet, and Taylor Reed is next up. We then move through the remaining draft order cards, showing the other participants in sequence with their current draft state before the first pick is made. This shows the opening draft board and the live turn sequence before the clock starts."

### Screen alignment notes
- Start on the app landing view with Mock current user visible.
- Use the Signed in as dropdown and select Fantasy Owner.
- Click Switch account immediately after selection.
- Scroll down on the dashboard to reveal the draft controls.
- Click Seed fantasy draft.
- Scroll down further to reveal the draft order cards.
- Click Sunset Open button after the draft loads.
- Pause for 2 seconds.
- Highlight the Fantasy Owner row and the On the clock label.
- Highlight the Taylor Reed row and the Next up label.
- Scroll or focus through the remaining draft order cards showing each participant with 0 of 4 drafted and No picks yet.
- End on the opening draft board with the live turn order visible before the first pick begins.

## Approval criteria

These copies are approved only when:
- the spoken wording matches the visible screen state,
- the sequence of user actions matches the Playwright capture,
- no line references a screen or action not present in the sequence,
- each part ends on the intended final state without adding extra narrative beats.

## Recording notes
- Record each part as a separate take.
- Keep the pacing deliberate and clean.
- Do not add optional ad-lib narration outside the approved script.
- Use the same voice and cadence for all three parts for consistency.
